How to play

The grid is split into pens — the areas ringed in a heavy line.

  • Every row, every column and every pen holds exactly the same number of bulls.
  • No two bulls may touch — not even at a corner.
  • Tap a cell to cycle it: empty → your own "no bull" note → 🐂 bull → empty.
  • Nothing can be lost. Undo takes back any move; Hint shows the next move the rules actually force, and tells you why.
